About Me

Get to know me better

I'm a dedicated Full-Stack Software Engineer currently at Dream71 Bangladesh Limited, specializing in enterprise-level solutions including ERP, CRM, and HRM systems. I hold a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering (BSc CSE) from Northern University Bangladesh and a Diploma in Computer Technology from Government Polytechnic.

With 4+ years of professional experience, I specialize in building scalable backend systems using Python, Django, and FastAPI, with strong expertise in microservices, event-driven architecture, distributed systems, and database design. I also develop AI-powered solutions using TensorFlow and OpenCV, along with modern frontend applications using Vue.js, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS. Focused on clean architecture, performance, and maintainable code, I build reliable ERP, CRM, and enterprise-grade systems that are scalable and production-ready.
